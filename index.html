💌 My Dearest Jaanu...

I really don't know how to start this letter...

First of all... I'm really, really sorry.

Till today, I never imagined that I would do something like this for any girl in my life. But today, I'm doing it because you mean that much to me.

The place you hold in my heart is something I can never explain in words.

What happened today... I still don't understand it myself. I never wanted to hurt you. That slap happened so suddenly, and if I could go back to that one second, I would change it without thinking twice.

Thank you for staying with me in class. Even when the people I once called my best friends slowly walked away, you stayed. Maybe you don't even know how much that meant to me.

Sometimes you get angry with me... Sometimes I act possessive... But honestly, I never mean anything seriously. I only tease you, joke around, and try to make everyone laugh.

There are a few moments when your sudden anger or a few words spoken in front of everyone quietly hurt my little heart. Maybe you had your own reasons. I'm not blaming you. I just wanted you to know how I feel.

We've been together since P1. That's such a long journey. We've shared laughs, silly fights, beautiful memories, and countless moments.

I don't want one bad moment to erase all of those memories.

I truly hope this beautiful bond stays with us forever.

I wish you become successful, achieve every dream, and always keep smiling.

One small request... Please be open with me. If you're angry, tell me. If I make a mistake, scold me. But please don't stay silent.

I know you're carrying your own pain. I may never completely understand it, but I'll always pray that life gives you nothing but happiness.

Once again... I'm truly, deeply sorry, Jaanu.

Thank you for every smile. Thank you for every memory. Thank you for simply being YOU.

I love you sooo much... as my best friend. ❤️
